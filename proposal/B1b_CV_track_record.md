## Narrative: track record and independence (~½ page)
**[Draft]:** My work runs from the circuit level (identifying the presynaptic elements and
opponent mechanisms of fly motion detection) through connectomics (MESH, the first
connectome-scale search for electrical synapses in the fly visuomotor pathway) to
evolution (the stepwise origin of insect eye movements). I conceived the gap-junction
search strategy and built the software independently. HOVER is a new direction that I
define and lead: it moves from describing one circuit to asking *why* circuits differ
between the sexes, and whether those differences hold lessons for machines. Throughout
I have translated research into open, participatory science **[add numbers: events,
participants, students, kits]**.
