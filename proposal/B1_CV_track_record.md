## Research independence and capacity to deliver HOVER (≈ ½ page)

*[Draft; rewrite in your own words]:* My work runs from circuits (the presynaptic elements and opponent
mechanisms of fly motion detection) through connectomics (MESH, the first connectome-scale search for
electrical synapses in the fly visuomotor pathway) to evolution (the stepwise origin of insect eye
movements). I conceived the gap-junction search strategy and built the software independently. HOVER
is a new programme that I define and lead from my own group at IU. It moves from describing one
circuit to asking *why* circuits differ between the sexes, and whether those differences generalise
to machines. Throughout, I have turned research into open, participatory science
