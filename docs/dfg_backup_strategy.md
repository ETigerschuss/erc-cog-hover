## 3. ERC re-application

- Under WP 2027, a B or C at Step 1 bars the applicant from the next year's ERC calls, and a C from
  two years earlier also bars. A not invited, or A/B at Step 2, carries no restriction (IfA p. 13).
  WP 2028 sets the rules for 2027 outcomes; expect the same pattern.
- A PhD from 2016 stays inside the CoG window until about **ERC-2031**, if the 5–15-year rule is kept
  (each call counts back from 1 January of its call year). Check each Work Programme.
- A resubmission is evaluated as a new proposal. Earlier reviews are not passed to the new reviewers
  (IfA p. 15).

## 4. Check DFG eligibility at IU now, not in July ⚠️

DFG eligibility for researchers at **private universities is not automatic**. The DFG has specific
criteria for institutions that are privately run or not exclusively non-profit, and IU is a private,
for-profit operator.

- Ask IU's research office whether any IU professor has held a DFG Sachbeihilfe; if unclear, ask
  the DFG directly.
- If IU staff are not eligible, build the backup around **LMU**: a joint Sachbeihilfe with an LMU
  co-applicant (e.g. R. Merrill) who hosts the positions, with the Fenk lab as cooperation partner.
  Researchers at Max Planck Institutes can apply to the DFG only under its cooperation requirement,
  so MPI-BI cannot be the anchor.
