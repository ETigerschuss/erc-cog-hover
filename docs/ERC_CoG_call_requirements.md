# ERC Consolidator Grant: rules that shape this proposal

> ⚠️ **Verify everything here against the current ERC Work Programme and the
> "Information for Applicants" for the call you submit to.** This page was written
> from general knowledge of recent CoG calls (WP 2024–2026). erc.europa.eu could not
> be reached from the drafting environment. Numbers marked *(verify)* change between
> work programmes.

## Eligibility

| Item | Typical rule | Our situation |
|---|---|---|
| Time since PhD | 7 to 12 years before 1 January of the call year. Documented extensions are possible (e.g., ~18 months per child for mothers, actual leave for fathers, long illness, clinical training) *(verify)* | PhD (LMU / Borst lab) date → **fill in** in the checklist |
| Host institution | Any legal entity **established in an EU Member State or Horizon Europe Associated Country** | "IU" must be confirmed. IU Internationale Hochschule (DE) is eligible as a legal entity. Indiana University (US) is **not**. |
| Time commitment | PI spends ≥ 40 % of working time on the project and ≥ 50 % of working time in an EU/AC country *(verify)* | Fits a professorship, but buy-out of teaching should be negotiated |
| Resubmission | Restrictions apply after a "B" (or "C") score at step 1/2 in the previous call *(verify)* | First ERC submission? → confirm |
| One proposal | Only one ERC frontier-research proposal per call (StG/CoG/AdG/SyG as PI) | — |

## Budget and duration

- Up to **€2.0 M for 5 years** *(verify)*, pro rata for shorter projects.
- Up to **€1.0 M additional** for (a) start-up costs of a PI moving into the EU/AC,
  (b) major equipment, (c) access to large facilities. Must be justified.
- 25 % flat-rate indirect costs on top of direct costs (within the cap).

Format: Times New Roman / Arial / Calibri ≥ 11 pt, single line spacing, A4, margins ≥ 2 cm *(verify)*.

## Evaluation

A single criterion: **scientific excellence**, assessed at two steps.

- **Step 1**: panel reads B1 only → A / B / C.
- **Step 2**: B2 is read by remote referees, then a **~30 min interview in Brussels
  (or online)**. CoG applicants are interviewed. The usual format is a ~10 min
  presentation followed by Q&A.

Review questions (wording approximate):

1. **Research project**: ground-breaking nature, ambition and feasibility.
   - Does it address important challenges at the frontier of the field(s)?
   - Are the objectives ambitious and beyond the state of the art?
   - Is the methodology appropriate (incl. high-risk/high-gain elements with suitable mitigation)?
   - Are the timescale and resources appropriate?
2. **Principal Investigator**: intellectual capacity, creativity and commitment.
   Since WP2024, the PI is assessed on demonstrated capacity to do ground-breaking
   research, using the narrative CV and track record. Do **not** use journal impact
   factors. Show the **independence** that a CoG requires.

## Panels relevant to HOVER

- **LS5**: Neuroscience and disorders of the nervous system (neural circuits, systems neuroscience, computational neuroscience)
- **PE6**: Computer science and informatics (computer vision, machine learning, neuromorphic computing)
- Secondary panel / cross-panel review can be requested. Interdisciplinary proposals
  go to one primary panel, which may request extra reviewers.
