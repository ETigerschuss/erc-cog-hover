# ERC-2027-CoG: the rules that shape HOVER

Source: *Information for Applicants to the Starting and Consolidator Grant 2027 Calls* (IfA),
version 11.0, 22 July 2026. Page numbers below refer to it. The ERC Consolidator Grant web page
was also used. The legally binding texts are the ERC Work Programme 2027 and the Model Grant
Agreement (MGA). Check the call page on the Funding & Tenders Portal (F&T) for updates and the FAQ.

Submit a first complete version **at least 48 h before the deadline**. Only the last version
submitted before the deadline is evaluated (p. 34, 37).

## Eligibility

- **PhD window:** the first PhD was successfully *defended* more than 5 and at most 15 years before
  1 Jan 2027, i.e. **1 Jan 2012 – 31 Dec 2021**, inclusive (p. 11). The defence date counts, not the
  award date (p. 10). Extensions are possible for documented circumstances (p. 57–61).
- **Host:** any legal entity established in an EU Member State or Associated Country, public or
  private. It must engage the PI for the project duration (p. 9–10).
- **Gender Equality Plan:** public bodies, research organisations and higher-education
  establishments, *including private ones*, need a GEP or an equivalent document in place at grant
  signature. It must meet four mandatory requirements: (1) published and signed by top management,
  (2) dedicated resources, (3) sex/gender-disaggregated data with reporting at least every two years,
  (4) training. It is self-declared in the Participant Register (p. 10, 25).
- **Time commitment:** ≥ 40 % of working time on the project and ≥ 50 % of working time in the EU/AC
  (p. 11, 26). The PI's personnel cost in the budget cannot exceed the declared percentage.
- **CoG benchmark:** "A competitive Consolidator Grant PI should have already shown evidence of
  research independence" (p. 9).
- **Novelty:** propose "truly novel ideas, not just continuations of ongoing work or existing
  collaboration" (p. 7).

## Submission restrictions (p. 12–13)

- Only one proposal to the WP 2027 calls, and only one ERC proposal under evaluation at any time.
- Outcome-based restrictions in WP 2027 (applied to earlier calls): a 2026 StG/CoG with **B or C at
  Step 1** cannot apply to any 2027 StG, CoG, AdG, Plus or SyG call. A **2025 C at Step 1** is also
  barred. **A not invited** and **A or B at Step 2** carry no restriction. Withdrawn, inadmissible and
  ineligible proposals do not count.
- → For HOVER, WP 2028 will set the consequences of the 2027 outcome. If it keeps this pattern, a
  B or C at Step 1 means no ERC application in 2028, and a C means none in 2029 either.

## Budget (p. 6, 8, 29–31, 66)

- Up to **€2,000,000 for 5 years**, pro rata for shorter projects. Up to **€1,000,000 additional**
  for PIs already in the EU/AC, which must be fully justified as necessary.
- 100 % of direct costs, plus a **25 % flat rate** for indirect costs on direct costs excluding
  subcontracting (B) and internally invoiced goods and services (D).
- Categories: **A** personnel (PI, senior staff, postdocs, students, other) · **B** subcontracting ·
  **C.1** travel · **C.2** equipment (depreciation; full capitalised cost only exceptionally and if
  listed) · **C.3** other goods, works and services (consumables, publications/open access,
  dissemination, other additional direct costs such as the certificate on financial statements) ·
  **D** internally invoiced · **E** indirect.
- **Resources text box: max 8000 characters.** Where it conflicts with the budget table, the table
  prevails. In-kind contributions from third parties must be declared.
- Open-access fees are eligible only for fully open-access venues (p. 31). Care costs (e.g.
  childcare during fieldwork) can be eligible (p. 30).

## What is evaluated when (p. 15–18, 27–29)

| | Step 1 | Step 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Material read | **B1 only:** Part I (5 pp) + CV and Track Record (4 pp) | Everything: B1 + **B2 Part II (7 pp)** + Part A budget, Resources and time commitment |
| Readers | Panel members acting as **generalists**, plus remote reviewers from other panels | Panel + specialist remote reviewers |
| Scores | A invited / A not invited / B / C | A (funded in rank order) / B |
| Interview | — | ≈ 30 min, applicant joins remotely. Presentation + Q&A, including budget questions. Format is set by each panel. |

Up to 44 proposals per panel are retained for Step 2 (p. 15).

### Evaluation questions (p. 18)

**Research project.** Step 1: Does the research address important scientific questions? Are the
objectives ambitious, does the project advance the frontier of knowledge, and is the approach
scientifically convincing? Step 2 adds: are the methodology and **working arrangements**
appropriate, and are the **timescales and resources** adequate and properly justified?

**PI (both steps).** Demonstrated ability to conduct ground-breaking research? Evidence of creative
and original thinking? The required scientific expertise and capacity to execute the project?
Ratings are Exceptional / Excellent / Very Good / Good / Non-competitive.

## Documents and limits (p. 24–33)

| Part | Content | Limit | Repo file |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part A (online form) | Acronym (≤ 20 chars), title, duration, primary + optional secondary panel, ≤ 4 ERC keywords, abstract; declarations; participants; budget table + Resources; ethics and security tables; other questions (PhD date, time commitment, consents, up to 3 excluded reviewers) | form | `proposal/abstract.md`, `proposal/PartA_budget_resources.md`, `admin/` |
| B1 cover page | PI, host, title, acronym, abstract (≈ ½ page, identical to Part A), duration in months; for cross-panel proposals, the extra panel(s) and why | not counted | `proposal/B1_cover_page.md` |
| B1 Part I of the Scientific Proposal | (1) state of knowledge, (2) scientific question and objectives, (3) overall approach | **5 pages**, excluding cover and references | `proposal/B1_PartI_synopsis.md` |
| B1 CV and Track Record | One template: personal details, education, key qualifications, positions, **PhD supervisor(s) and postdoc mentor(s)**, up to **10 research outputs** (recent emphasis) with significance and your role, **selected peer recognition** with why it matters. Optional: career breaks, diverse paths, life events, contributions to the community. | **4 pages** | `proposal/B1_CV_track_record.md` |
| B2 Part II of the Scientific Proposal | Methodology, work plan, risks and mitigation, further background. Must **not repeat Part I** and must match the Part A Resources. | **7 pages**, excluding references | `proposal/B2_PartII.md` |
| B2 Funding ID | Current grants and their subjects, plus any **ongoing application for related work** | not counted | end of `proposal/B2_PartII.md` |
| Annexes (PDF) | PhD certificate showing the **defence date** (or the awarding institution's confirmation, Annex 4.9); **Host Institution Support Letter** (mandatory wording); ethics/security documents if needed; extension request if needed | separate | `admin/` |

**Format (p. 27, 36):** use the official B1/B2 templates from the submission system. A4; Times New
Roman, Arial or similar, ≥ 11 pt; single line spacing; margins 2 cm at the sides and 1.5 cm at the
bottom. **Every page carries a header with the PI's last name, the acronym and the part
(B1/B2).** PDFs ≤ 10 MB each (aim for ≤ 2 MB for B1 and B2); alphanumeric file names without spaces
or special characters. Reviewers need not follow links. Support letters, brochures and other
extra documents are **disregarded** (p. 33).

**Author lists must appear exactly as published**, with joint first or corresponding authorship
marked (p. 28). Misreporting is treated as misrepresentation (p. 8).

## Declarations and integrity

- **Written consent** must exist *before the deadline* from every researcher named in the proposal:
  team members, collaborators and advisory-board members. ERCEA may ask for proof (p. 24, 26).
- No contact with panel members or reviewers about the evaluation (p. 14).
- **AI:** external help, including AI, may be used to prepare a proposal, but the PI keeps full
  authorship responsibility (acknowledgements, plagiarism, good conduct). All proposals are screened
  with plagiarism-detection software (p. 8, 18).

## Ethics and security (p. 19–20, 26)

- The ethics issues table must be completed even when nothing applies. Areas include human
  participants, personal data, animals, non-EU countries, environment/health/safety and
  **artificial intelligence**. The research must have an **exclusive focus on civil applications**.
- Security issues table (YES/NO for each question): classified information, potential misuse of
  results, security restrictions.
- → Draft: `admin/ethics_security_self_assessment.md`.

## Panel and keywords (Annex 4.1)

- **Primary: LS5**, Neuroscience and Disorders of the Nervous System ("systems neuroscience and
  modelling … in humans and all other organisms").
- **Secondary: PE6**, Computer Science and Informatics. The B1 cover page must justify it.
- Keywords (max 4; keyword 1 is mandatory):
  1. **LS5_16** Systems and computational neuroscience (e.g. modelling, simulation, brain oscillations, connectomics)
  2. **LS5_7** Sensory systems, sensation and perception
  3. **PE6_14** New computational paradigms, quantum computing, bio-inspired computing
  4. **PE6_11** Computer vision, computer graphics, visualization
  - Alternative to 4: **LS8_11** Behavioural ecology and evolution, if the evolutionary/dimorphism angle leads.

## Practicalities (p. 35–38)

- Use an EU Login account, **not** the "eID sign-in" option (that account cannot edit or submit).
- The person who creates the draft manages access. Only one person should edit the forms at a time
  ("last save wins").
- Choose IU's correct **PIC** at Step 3. The PI and host contact emails entered at Step 4 are read-only
  in the form later, so use your IU address.
- Contacts: ERC-2027-COG-APPLICANTS@ec.europa.eu · ethics: ERC-ETHICS-REVIEW@ec.europa.eu ·
  the German ERC National Contact Point (NKS ERC) for personal advice.
