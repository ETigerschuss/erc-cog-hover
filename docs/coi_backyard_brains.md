# Conflict-of-interest plan: Backyard Brains

You are affiliated with Backyard Brains (BYB), which makes SpikerBot and SpikeStation, and HOVER uses
these products in WP5. That is allowed, but it must be **visible and managed**. Disclosed with
safeguards, it is a strength: an existing route into thousands of classrooms. Undisclosed, it can
look like misrepresentation, and the CV has to list your current positions anyway (IfA p. 28).

## Rules that apply

- **Horizon Europe MGA, "Conflict of interests" article** (Art. 12 of the general MGA; confirm with
  IU's grants office). The beneficiary (IU) must take measures to prevent situations where the
  impartial and objective implementation of the grant is compromised by economic or other interests.
- **Purchases** must follow IU's usual purchasing practice, give best value for money and avoid
  conflicts of interest (MGA cost-eligibility rules).
- **In-kind contributions** from third parties must be declared in the Resources section (IfA p. 66).
- **Results:** the ERC may object to an exclusive licence or transfer of ownership of results for up
  to 4 years after the project (IfA p. 7, fn 9). Open licences avoid this issue entirely.

## Safeguards to write into the proposal and agree with IU

1. **Disclose** the role in three places:
   - the CV (positions);
   - one sentence in B2, WP5;
   - one sentence in the Resources section.

   Suggested wording: *"The PI holds a [role] at Backyard Brains, the manufacturer of SpikerBot.
   Robot hardware is procured by IU without the PI's involvement, and all HOVER software runs on at
   least one open-hardware alternative."*
2. **Procurement.** IU procurement buys robots against a hardware-neutral specification: camera,
   on-board spiking-neuron runtime, motor control, unit price, classroom robustness. It collects
   competing quotes, and you are recused from the decision.
   *Alternative:* BYB donates robots as an in-kind contribution, declared with its value in Resources.
3. **No BYB subcontracting** of HOVER tasks. BYB staff are paid from the grant only through IU's
   normal open recruitment.
4. **Open results.** Firmware, circuit files and teaching kits are released under MIT/CC-BY, and
   ported to at least one other platform (ESP32 or Raspberry Pi robot). BYB gets no exclusive rights.
5. **Time.** Declare your BYB time to IU. Check that ≥ 40 % on HOVER and ≥ 50 % in the EU/AC still hold.
6. **Papers.** Add a competing-interest statement to every paper that uses BYB hardware.
7. **Application account.** Use your IU e-mail for the EU Login account and Part A, not a company address.
8. **Interview answer** (prepare it). *Why SpikerBot?* It is the lowest-cost classroom robot with a
   camera that runs spiking neurons natively, and it has an existing teacher network. HOVER's code is
   hardware-neutral, so the choice can be replaced.
